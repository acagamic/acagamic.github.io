I joined the Security Advisory Board of IMAGINE, a Horizon Europe project using gamification and digital storytelling for youth-crime prevention. I serve alongside Prof. Jérôme Ferret (University of Toulouse Capitole / TSE), Prof. Marco Lombardi (Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, Milan), and Prof. Dina Siegel (Utrecht University), with Andrew Staniforth, IMAGINE's Project Security Officer, leading the board. [Public project](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101308662)
