_First published as The Acagamic Tip Tuesday #7 on acagamic.com. Edited for this archive._

> Avoid putting too much cognitive load on the player when engaging in game actions. People are bad at multitasking.

Show complex information during a break, a pause, or after a match. Games without real-time action, such as turn-based strategy games, can show more, because players have time to process it before they move.
