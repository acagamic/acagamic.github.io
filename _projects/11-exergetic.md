EXERGETIC developed exergame training to improve physical and cognitive function in older adults, designed for realistic and safe use outside the lab. The project was funded through the CIHR Active and Assisted Living Program. Related publications include [Exergame (ExerG)-Based Physical-Cognitive Training for Rehabilitation](https://doi.org/10.2196/73405) and [ExerCube vs. Virtual Reality](https://doi.org/10.1145/3748603).

- **Role:** Funded project
- **Funder:** CIHR Active and Assisted Living Program
- **Years:** 2020 to 2023
