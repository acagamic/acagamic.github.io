SWaGUR (Saskatchewan-Waterloo Games User Research) was an NSERC CREATE training program, led by Regan Mandryk, that prepared graduate students for careers in games user research. Lennart was a co-applicant, and trainees learned research methods such as those covered in [Games User Research Methods](https://doi.org/10.1145/3270316.3271548) and the [biometric measures chapter](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198794844.003.0016) of the Games User Research book.

- **Role:** Co-applicant
- **Funder:** NSERC CREATE
- **Years:** 2016 to 2022
