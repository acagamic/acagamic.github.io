Diagnosing and treating eye disease in young children depends on keeping them engaged through tests and exercises. Through the Waterloo-Huawei Joint Innovation Lab, this project, led by Lennart with co-investigators Daniel Vogel and Ben Thompson, developed gameful approaches to diagnosis, therapy and prevention of eye disease in young children.

- **Role:** Principal investigator
- **Funder:** Waterloo-Huawei Joint Innovation Lab
- **Years:** 2021 to 2024
