Avatars are how people appear to each other in social VR, and customizing them can change how people feel about themselves and others. Funded through a Facebook/Meta research award, this project took a critical look at the psychological effects of custom avatars in social VR. Lennart was a co-applicant.

- **Role:** Co-applicant
- **Funder:** Facebook/Meta Awards
- **Years:** 2022 to 2024
