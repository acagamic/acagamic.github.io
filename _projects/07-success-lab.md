SUCCESS (the Seamless User-Centred Conscious Experience Study System) is research infrastructure funded by the Canada Foundation for Innovation's John R. Evans Leaders Fund. It equips the lab to study user experience in depth, including physiological and behavioural measures that complement what participants report. Lennart led it with co-investigator Leah Zhang-Kennedy.

- **Role:** Principal investigator
- **Funder:** Canada Foundation for Innovation, John R. Evans Leaders Fund
- **Years:** 2021 to 2026
