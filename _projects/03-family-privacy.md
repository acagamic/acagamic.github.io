Game consoles sit in family rooms and collect data from everyone who plays, including children. This project treats privacy as something families practise together and examines how Canadian children's privacy is negotiated around console gaming. It builds on the lab's research on privacy and deceptive design in games, such as [Immersive Invaders](https://doi.org/10.1145/3757612). Lennart is a co-applicant on this SSHRC-funded project.

- **Role:** Co-applicant
- **Funder:** SSHRC Bob Harding and Lois Claxton Humanities and Social Sciences Endowment Fund
- **Years:** 2026 to present
