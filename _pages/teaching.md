Lennart teaches user experience research, game design, and research writing in the [Master of Digital Experience Innovation](https://uwaterloo.ca/stratford-school-of-interaction-design-and-business/) and the Global Business and Digital Arts programs at the Stratford School, plus graduate seminars in Systems Design Engineering and Computer Science.

## Current course

**GBDA 410: User Experience Research: People and Generative AI Systems** (Fall 2026). Students study how people interact with generative systems that create content and take actions. Topics include instruction, delegation, trust, evaluation from real system errors, synthetic participants, and governance. Students design and defend original studies.

## Courses and teaching resources

- [User Experience Design and Research in Games](/teaching/chi2023-course/), an online course at ACM CHI 2023, taught with Pejman Mirza-Babaei and Anders Drachen, with videos and slides.
- [Tokens, the small pieces a model reads](https://tokentutorial.lennartnacke.com), an interactive tutorial on how language models split text into tokens, read them in order, and write a reply one token at a time. Readers type their own text and watch each token light up.
